# Licensing and Notices Policy

## Current status
No repository-wide license is declared yet. Do not assume MOMO-owned files or the repository as a whole are licensed for redistribution until an explicit license is added by the maintainer.

## Third-party code
Each component retains its original license unless a valid relicensing arrangement exists. Before copying, modifying, distributing, or linking a component:
- Read the license at the exact pinned revision.
- Preserve copyright and license notices.
- Satisfy source-offer, attribution, patent, trademark, and notice requirements.
- Check licenses of bundled dependencies and generated artifacts.
- Confirm whether firmware blobs and vendor files may legally be redistributed.

## Android ecosystem
Android platform components, Linux kernel components, device-specific code, vendor files, and third-party apps may have different licenses. Review them independently; never treat the whole platform as having one license.

## Required records
Maintain a dependency inventory with component, upstream URL, pinned revision, license identifier, modifications, notices, and redistribution status.

## Release gate
No public binary or source bundle may be released until licensing review and required notices are complete.
